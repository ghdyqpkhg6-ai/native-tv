Privacy policy

This applies to the personal Native Tv build at this site, used on one TV by its owner.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google's sign-in for TVs, it reads the YouTube account's list of subscribed channels and those channels' newest public videos (read-only scope youtube.readonly). It does not read watch history, search history, comments or any other account data, and it cannot change anything in the account.

Where data is kept

Only on the TV itself: a Google refresh token and the last list of videos, in a file readable only by the TV's system user. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access

Choose Sign out on the TV, which revokes the access with Google and deletes the file, or remove "Native Tv" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
